User problem

  1. Designed the rules and the game economy balance: two iterations of card balance.

Parole

About the product
A word list is pasted from a spreadsheet or from ChatGPT, and the app shows words one by one. Three modes: Italian to Russian, Russian to Italian, both ways. A word counts as learned after several correct answers in a row.
User problem
A simple way to learn Italian words from your own lists without a paid Apple developer account.
My role
Product Owner: lesson flow, word import format, rules for a “learned” word, publishing without the App Store.
What I did
  1. Word import: pasted list, csv file, cells from Google Sheets and Excel. The language is detected automatically.
  2. Lesson: three modes, mistakes return in the same lesson, letter-by-letter hint, “Count as correct” button.
  3. Ready-made ChatGPT prompts that return words in the right format.
  4. Published as a site that installs to the Home screen and works offline.
Result
  • Working app: installs to the Home screen, works offline.
  • Built for personal use. No audience data.
